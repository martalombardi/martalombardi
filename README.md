# Hi, I'm Marta 👋🏻

I hold an **MSc in Data Science** from *Sapienza University of Rome* and a **BSc in Computer Science** from the *University of Rome Tor Vergata*.

I am interested in using computational and data-driven methods to understand complex phenomena. My main research interests are **Complex Systems**, **Computational Social Science**, **Large Language Models** and **Deep Learning**.

### 🔬 Research

My research path started with my Bachelor's thesis on **opinion dynamics** and **information diffusion in networks**, where I used **stochastic processes**, **Markov chains** and **computational simulations** to study collective dynamics and convergence in the Voter Model.

My Master's thesis, **“Hierarchical Structural Signatures of Human and LLM-Generated Discourse”**, investigates how human and LLM-generated texts differ in their hierarchical organization. I combined **Rhetorical Structure Theory** and **grammar-based tree compression** to identify recurrent structural patterns, which were also used to estimate the **proportion of AI-generated content at the corpus level through mixture proportion estimation**.

### 💻 Background

My academic work has given me experience across **machine learning** and **deep learning**, **statistical** and **probabilistic modelling**, **natural language processing**, **network analysis**, **optimization**, **data mining**, **Big Data** and **cloud computing**, together with a solid foundation in **programming** and **algorithms**.

I particularly enjoy turning research questions into computational models, designing experiments, interpreting their results and seeing how each answer can lead to new questions.
