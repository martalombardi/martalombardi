# Hi, I'm Marta 👋🏻

I'm a **Data Science graduate from Sapienza University of Rome**, with a background in **Computer Science** from the University of Rome Tor Vergata.

I am interested in using computational and data-driven methods to understand complex phenomena. My main research interests are **Complex Systems, Computational Social Science, Large Language Models and Deep Learning**.

### 🔬 Research

My research path started with **opinion dynamics on networks**, studying how information spreads and how collective behaviour can emerge from local interactions.

For my Master's thesis, **“Hierarchical Structural Signatures of Human and LLM-Generated Discourse”**, I studied how human and LLM-generated texts differ in the way they organize information, using hierarchical discourse representations and compression-based methods.

### 💻 Background

My academic work has given me experience across **machine learning and deep learning, statistical and probabilistic modelling, natural language processing, network analysis, optimization, data mining, Big Data and cloud computing**, together with a solid foundation in programming and algorithms.

I particularly enjoy turning research questions into computational models, implementing methods from the literature, designing experiments and analysing their results.
